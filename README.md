hi, i'm TX from italy. most of what i do is for [**Spicy Lyrics**](https://github.com/spicylyrics): i work on the apps, the TTML tool, and the guides, and i sync a lot of lyrics myself.

i'm a perfectionist who procrastinates way too much and i'm kinda socially awkward, but feel free to message me, i like talking to people.

### 🎤 main stuff

- **[spicy lyrics mobile](https://github.com/spicylyrics/mobile)**: word-synced lyrics on android for whatever you're playing (spotify, yt music, pretty much any player)
- **[spicy lyrics ttml tool](https://github.com/spicylyrics/ttml-tool)**: the editor we use to sync lyrics word by word. there's a [web version](https://tool.spicylyrics.org) and desktop builds. fork of AMLL TTML Tool, maintained with NaeNae

### 🧪 other projects

- **[spotmatch](https://github.com/TheX24/SpotMatch)**: finds every spotify ID for the same song (there's usually more than one)
- **[spicy wallpaper](https://github.com/TheX24/spicy-wallpaper)**: wallpaper engine wallpaper that syncs to what you're listening to
- **[ttml-review](https://github.com/TheX24/ttml-review)**: checks a TTML against the spicy lyrics guides
- **[spicy-player-old](https://github.com/TheX24/spicy-player-old)**: my old offline music player, not developed anymore
